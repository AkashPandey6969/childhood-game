# Privacy Policy for Gem Delver

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

**Gem Delver** ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a standalone, offline puzzle game developed by the Gem Delver Team. We are deeply committed to protecting player privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection

**Gem Delver collects no personal information whatsoever.**

- **No Personal Data**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not integrate any tracking services, analytics libraries, crash-reporting SDKs, or behavioral monitors (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or Unity Analytics).
- **No Advertisements**: The game contains no third-party ad networks, banners, interstitials, or tracking cookies.
- **No Accounts Required**: You do not need to register an account, sign in, or connect with social media services to enjoy the entire game.

---

## 2. Local Device Storage

All game progress—including unlocked levels, earned star ratings, best move scores, and audio preferences—is stored exclusively on your local device:
- On Web browsers, data is stored in your local browser storage (`localStorage`).
- On Android, data is stored within your device's private app sandbox.

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted over the internet. You can erase all saved data at any time by selecting "Reset Data" in the game's Main Menu or by clearing the app data in your device's Android settings.

---

## 3. Network & Internet Permissions

While Capacitor or Android platform defaults may include network capabilities (e.g. `android.permission.INTERNET`), the game operates 100% offline. No telemetry, gameplay data, or user information is transmitted to any external server.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Gem Delver is suitable for all ages and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations:
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).
- The game contains no chat functions, multi-player interactions, external links, advertisements, or data collection mechanisms.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Because the game does not collect any data, we do not anticipate needing to modify this policy. However, if our practices change in future updates, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document and provide notice in the game's release notes.
