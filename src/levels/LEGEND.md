# Gem Delver Level Map Legend

## Core Symbols (Phase 2 & 3)
- `#`: Indestructible Wall
- `.`: Walkable Floor
- `P`: Player Start Position (1 per level)
- `E`: Exit Door (unlocked when all diamonds are collected)
- `*`: Collectible Diamond

## Puzzle Mechanics (Phase 4)
- `B`: Pushable Block
- `k`: Key (picked up into inventory)
- `D`: Door (consumed when player walks into it with a key)
- `s`: Pressure Plate / Switch (held open by player or block)
- `G`: Gate (open while linked switch is held down)
- `O`: Hole (walkable once filled by pushing a block into it)

## Hazards & Items (Phase 5)
- `^`: Spikes (lethal unless wearing spike boots)
- `R`: Rolling Boulder
- `S`: Simple Patrolling Enemy
- `c`: Cracked Wall (breakable with pickaxe)
- `b`: Bush (burnable with fire item)
